		It’s a static website serving the complete text of <em>Webster’s Revised Unabridged Dictionary from 1913</em>.
	</p>
	<p>
		James Somers wrote <a href="https://jsomers.net/blog/dictionary">a popular blog post</a> about <em>Webster’s 1913</em>, explaining how its definitions are more vivid and literary than what you’ll find in a modern dictionary.
	</p>
	<p>
		There already exists another site, that I sometimes use, <a href="https://www.websters1913.com/">websters1913.com</a>, but unfortunately it has an SSL cert issue, and is not very feature-rich.
	</p>
